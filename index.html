Your progress is earned under the bar

Over9 logs your sets in a few taps, tracks your records, and raises your level — only when you actually train.

Coming soon to Google Play · Android

Your session, logged in a few taps

Start a workout: each exercise opens pre-filled with your last session. Adjust a weight or the reps, then save. The plate calculator tells you what to load on each side of the bar. Each exercise can show your own image: a photo you take with your phone, or any image you choose.

Your level, and what's left

The home screen shows your level and how many sessions stand between you and the next one. A session is a day you actually trained: opening the app doesn't count.

27 levels. None for sale.

Every level is earned under the bar, one session after another — and your aura changes along the way.

Your records, charted

Every set you log feeds the exercise's records and its chart over time: heaviest weight, most reps, and the goal you set yourself.

A 3D body that reads your training

It shows where your weekly volume stands against the target of ten sets per muscle group, which muscles are lagging, where you are progressing, and any pain you report. And when you don't know what to train, it suggests today's session.

Your history, day by day

The calendar marks your training days, and every session keeps its sets, weights, and reps.

Your data is yours

No account required: the app runs on your phone, even offline. If you turn on sync, a copy goes to your own Google Drive, and nowhere else.

No trackers, no ads

No advertising and no analytics, in the app or on this site.

Paid once

A single purchase. No subscription, nothing to unlock later.

In 9 languages

The app follows your phone's language, or the one you pick for it.
