Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 12, 2026

In short. Your data stays on your phone. If you turn on sync, a copy goes to your own Google Drive. We receive none of your data: no server of ours, no account with us, no advertising, no analytics, no trackers. You can delete everything yourself, at any time.

Who we are

This policy covers the Limit Breaker Android app, distributed on Google Play, and this website, limitbreakerfit.com. Both are published by Jean-Philippe Royer, an independent developer in Quebec, Canada, who is also the person in charge of protecting personal information.

For any privacy question or request: contact@limitbreakerfit.com.

What the app stores on your phone

  • Your exercises, workouts and sessions: sets, weights, reps and other measurements, dates, notes, tags and favorites.
  • The pains you report: the affected area (a muscle or a joint), the date you report it and the date you clear it. This is health data: it stays under your control, just like everything else.
  • Your progress photos, if you take any, and the images you add to your exercises.
  • Your settings.
  • If you sign in with Google: your account's name, email address and profile photo address, to show who is signed in.

Everything is stored in the app's private space on your phone. We have no access to it.

The app uses the camera only when you take a progress photo. It needs no other sensitive permission.

Syncing with your Google Drive (optional)

If you connect your Google account and allow Drive, the app copies to your Drive, in a folder named LimitBreakerFitApp: your training data (including reported pains), your exercise images, your progress photos, and archives of their previous versions.

  • The permission requested is drive.file: the app sees and changes only the files it created itself. It has no access to the rest of your Drive.
  • Data travels directly between your phone and Google, encrypted in transit (HTTPS). It never goes through us.
  • This copy is kept by Google under its own terms and privacy policy.

Your Google account data

  • Access: through Sign in with Google, your name, email address and profile photo address; through the drive.file Drive permission, only the files the app created.
  • Use: showing the signed-in account, and saving then reading back the copy of your data in your Drive. Nothing else: no advertising, no profiling, no sale, no training of artificial intelligence models.
  • Storage: your name, email and photo address on your phone; your files in your Drive. The app stores no password.
  • Sharing: none. This data never reaches us, and the app passes it to no one.

What we don't do

  • No server of ours, no account to create with us.
  • No advertising.
  • No analytics, audience measurement or tracking tools, in the app or on this site.
  • No selling, renting or sharing of data.

Third-party services involved

  • Google Play handles purchase, refunds, installation and updates, under Google's privacy policy. Google sends us aggregated statistics on sales and usage. If you agreed to share diagnostics with Google, it also sends us crash reports, without your name or email address.
  • Android backup: if your phone's backup is on, Android may copy the app's data into your Google account. Your progress photos are excluded. This backup is managed by Google and your settings.

These services — Google, GitHub, Porkbun — may store data outside Quebec and Canada, notably in the United States.

If you email us

An email to contact@limitbreakerfit.com goes through our forwarding service (Porkbun) before reaching our mailbox (Gmail). It is the only personal data that ever reaches us. We use your address and message only to answer you, because you asked us to (under the GDPR, our legitimate interest in answering your request). We keep them for 12 months after our last exchange, then delete them, or sooner if you ask.

This website

limitbreakerfit.com uses no cookies, no analytics and no third-party content: fonts, images and styles are served by the site itself. It is hosted on GitHub Pages. As GitHub states, visitors' IP addresses are logged and stored for security purposes (GitHub documentation).

Retention and deletion

  • On your phone: your data stays until you delete it in the app or uninstall the app, which erases everything.
  • In your Drive: as long as you keep it there. Delete the LimitBreakerFitApp folder to erase it, and remove the app's access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Your emails: 12 months after our last exchange, or sooner on request.

We hold no other data about you.

Security

Android isolates the app's data in its private space. Exchanges with Google are encrypted. Access to your Drive goes through a Google permission you can withdraw at any time, and the app only sees its own files.

Governance

Limit Breaker is the work of a single person, who is also the person in charge of protecting personal information: no one else has access to the emails received. Any new feature that would touch your data is reviewed against this policy before release, and an incident affecting your information would be reported to you, and to the competent authority where the law requires it.

Your rights

Since your data stays with you, you view, correct, export (the files in your Drive are readable CSV files) and delete it yourself. For any question or request, including under the GDPR or Quebec's Act respecting the protection of personal information in the private sector, write to contact@limitbreakerfit.com: we answer within 30 days. You may also contact a data protection authority, such as the CNIL in France or the Commission d'accès à l'information in Quebec.

Children

Limit Breaker is not intended for children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top of the page changes with it. A significant change will also be announced in the app's release notes.
