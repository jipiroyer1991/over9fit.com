Support

A question, a bug, an idea: write to contact@limitbreakerfit.com. We answer in English or French.

How do I back up my sessions to Google Drive?

In the ☰ menu, open Settings and connect your Google account. The app then asks for access to your Drive: it only sees the files it creates itself, in a folder named LimitBreakerFitApp. Let automatic upload do its job, or tap Sync now.

Sync is optional: without it, everything stays on your phone.

I'm changing phones: how do I get my data back?

Install Limit Breaker on the new phone with the same Google Play account, connect the same Google account in Settings, and sync: your sessions come back from your Drive.

If sync was never turned on on the old phone, your data never left it: sync from the old phone first.

Does the app work offline?

Yes. Everything is logged and viewed offline; only syncing with Drive needs a connection.

How do I get a refund?

Purchases go through Google Play, which handles refunds. You can return the app yourself from Google Play within two hours of buying it. After that, Google reviews requests made within 48 hours. Past that, write to us at contact@limitbreakerfit.com.

How do I change the app's language?

The app follows your phone's language. You can also give it a different one without touching the rest of the phone: Settings → Apps → Limit Breaker → Language. Nine languages are available: English, French, German, Spanish, Italian, Portuguese, Japanese, Korean and Chinese.

How do I delete all my data?

On your phone: uninstalling the app deletes everything it keeps there.

On your Drive: the copy belongs to you; delete the LimitBreakerFitApp folder. To remove the app's access to your Google account: myaccount.google.com/permissions.

In your Android backup: if your phone's backup is on, Android may copy the app's data into it (never your progress photos). You manage it in your phone's backup settings.

We have nothing to delete on our side: your data never reaches us. The details are in the privacy policy.
